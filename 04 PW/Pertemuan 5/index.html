About Me

I am a Computer Engineering student at Universitas Pendidikan Indonesia with a diverse background in full-stack development, technical education, and engineering innovation. I have experience transforming static web pages into dynamic applications using TypeScript and React.js, and I have designed database schemas to support full CRUD operations. Beyond coding, I have a strong foundation in curriculum development, having created educational materials for digital literacy and cybersecurity. My leadership experience includes serving as the President of IT 24, where I led programs to advance ICT within a school environment.

I consider myself a "full-time hackathon junkie" and have won several prestigious awards, including being the Grand Champion at the FTSL Infrastructure Innovation Summit 2023 and taking first place in 3D Modelling at the Ink and Metal STEM Olympics. My technical versatility is also reflected in my certifications in DevOps, SQL, and Responsive Web Design. I enjoy applying my engineering skills to practical projects, such as building remote-controlled cars and developing hardware control logic for community service programs.
